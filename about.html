About TechVision 2026

TechVision 2026 is the annual technology event organized by our university. It brings together students, faculty and industry professionals to share knowledge, showcase projects and discuss the future of technology.

Our Mission

To inspire young minds, encourage innovation and build a strong link between academic learning and the technology industry.

Event Highlights

  • Keynote sessions by industry experts
  • Hands-on workshops on web development and deployment
  • Student project exhibition
  • Coding and design competitions
  • Career and networking sessions
